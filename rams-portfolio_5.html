Every frame begins with light.

As a VFX Lighting Artist, I transform digital environments into believable cinematic experiences through lighting, shading, and visual storytelling. As a Wildlife Photographer, I explore the natural world, capturing authentic moments that require patience, timing, and respect for nature.

These two disciplines constantly inspire one another. Nature teaches me realism, atmosphere, and color; visual effects allow me to reimagine those principles into extraordinary cinematic worlds.

This portfolio showcases my journey across both realities — where creativity, technology, and storytelling come together to create memorable visuals.

— Career / 01

One eye, two disciplines

Sairam Kumar Tammina is a VFX Lead currently at Untold Studios, with close to a decade in lighting and lookdev across Houdini, Maya, Arnold, Renderman, Mantra, and Octane. Career credits include Sonic the Hedgehog 3 (Paramount), Prey, and Thirteen Lives.

Outside the pipeline, wildlife photography is the counterweight: no render passes, no undo, just reading light as it happens. Both practices come from the same instinct — noticing how light behaves, and knowing when it's right.
